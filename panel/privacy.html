Token Panel · Privacy policy / 隐私政策

Updated / 更新：2026-11-03 · JVH

English

Keys stay in the local macOS Keychain; endpoint addresses, field mappings and display preferences stay on this Mac. Automatically fetched quota results stay in memory. Manually entered balances, limits, reset dates and update times are saved locally with your slot settings. The native quota reader does not inspect password fields, chat history, contacts or personal files. If you sign in through a provider web page, that page sends your sign-in information directly to the provider and may store cookies or other website data locally. JVH runs no server receiving app data, and the app has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK.

With preview off, the app sends HTTPS GET quota requests directly to your configured endpoint approximately every 60 seconds. The endpoint receives the Key when authentication is selected, plus network information such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. JVH does not receive the Key or quota response. Only connect to services you trust and are authorized to use.

Custom API requests do not follow redirects or store cookies, and do not generate model content. A custom service may still charge for queries; its rules apply.

Legacy preset adapters remain available: MiniMax uses its official regional Token Plan API; DeepSeek, Kimi and Qwen use official usage pages in temporary WebKit workers. Each web slot has isolated local website storage, which contains sign-in data under that provider’s policies. These integrations can depend on subscriptions, login status and page changes; no commercial affiliation or universal compatibility is claimed.

Preview sends no quota requests. Enable preview or quit to stop subsequent queries. Delete saved Keys in Settings; Panel also supports clearing a slot. Uninstalling does not guarantee removal of Keychain or website data. Local deletion does not revoke provider Keys; revoke them at the provider if needed.

If you contact support, the email address and information you send are used to handle your request. Do not send secrets. Request deletion of support correspondence at hesicun@msn.com, subject to legally required retention.

This static support website contains no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process access logs to deliver and secure it. Policy changes will update the date shown here.

简体中文

Key 保存在本机 macOS 钥匙串；接口地址、字段映射和显示偏好保存在本机，自动查询的额度结果保留在运行内存；手动输入的余额、总量、重置时间和更新时间随槽位设置保存在本机。原生额度读取器不检查密码输入框、聊天记录、通讯录或个人文件。通过厂商网页登录时，该网页会直接向厂商发送登录信息，并可能在本机保留 Cookie 或其他网站数据。JVH 不运行接收应用数据的服务器，应用不含广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

关闭预览后，应用约每 60 秒直接向你配置的地址发送 HTTPS GET 额度请求。选择认证时，服务方会收到 Key，以及 IP 等网络信息，并按其自身政策处理。JVH 不接收 Key 或额度响应。请仅连接你信任且有权使用的服务。

自定义 API 请求不跟随重定向、不保存 Cookie，也不生成模型内容。自定义服务仍可能对查询收费，以其规则为准。

原有预设适配器仍保留：MiniMax 使用所选区域官方 Token Plan API；DeepSeek、Kimi、Qwen 通过临时 WebKit 辅助进程读取官方用量页。每个网页槽位使用隔离的本机网站存储，包含按厂商政策处理的登录状态。这些功能受套餐、登录及网页改版影响，不代表官方合作或保证兼容所有服务。

预览不发起额度请求；开启预览或退出应用可停止后续查询。可在设置删除已保存 Key，Panel 还可清空槽位。卸载不保证清除钥匙串或网页登录数据。本机删除不等于厂商撤销，如需撤销请前往厂商操作。

主动联系支持时，你发送的邮箱及问题内容用于处理请求。请勿发送秘密。可通过 hesicun@msn.com 请求删除支持往来，依法须保留的信息除外。

此静态支持网站不含分析脚本、广告或追踪 Cookie。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，托管方可能为交付页面及保障安全处理访问日志。政策变更会更新本页日期。
